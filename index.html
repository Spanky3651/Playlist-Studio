How it works

Pick a search source, filter, Search, add the maps you want to Your Playlist, then export a .bplist. Two sources: BeatLeader for scores, PP and your play history, or BeatSaver to browse by tags and genres. You're not stuck with everything that matches — cherry-pick, mix results from several searches, drag rows to reorder, then export just your picks. Already know the maps you want? Paste their BSR codes into Quick Add.

In-Game Sync

Optional. Bakes a syncURL into the .bplist so PlaylistManager's in-game Sync button re-downloads the playlist from wherever you host it. Nothing is uploaded here — Studio just writes the address you paste.

Paste the address your .bplist will live at. It must serve the raw file, not a web page, and stay constant — if you move or rename it later, existing copies stop syncing.
